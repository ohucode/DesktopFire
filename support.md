# Desktop Fire Support

Desktop Fire is a menu bar app for macOS 14 or later.

## Getting started

1. Open the app, then click the flame icon in the menu bar.
2. Drag the hearth to your preferred spot.
3. Open **Customize** to choose your wood, hearth, flame color, size, and burn duration. Some appearance options require the Plus Pack.
4. Use the menu to hide or show the fire, extinguish it, or relight it.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why isn't the app in the Dock?

Desktop Fire is controlled from the menu bar. Look for the flame icon at the top of your screen.

### Why can't I see the fire?

Check its visibility and selected display in the menu bar. If **Always on Top** is off, other windows may cover it. Choose **Bring Fire to Front** to reveal it. Enable **Show on All Desktops** to see it across desktops.

### Why does the fire fade near my pointer?

The fire becomes more transparent when your pointer lingers nearby to keep it out of your way. Move the pointer away to restore it. It remains fully visible while you drag the hearth or use its menu.

### Why are only ashes left?

The fire goes out naturally when the selected burn duration ends. Choose **Relight Fire** to start again. Burn time continues while the fire is hidden or your Mac sleeps. Quitting and reopening the app starts a new burn.

### Why can't I hear crackling?

The current version does not include audio.

### What is included for free, and what does Plus add?

Oak, Stone Ring, the original flame color, and unlimited relighting are free. The Plus Pack is a one-time in-app purchase that adds five woods, eight hearths, and every flame color. It is not a subscription.

### My Plus purchase isn't available. What should I do?

Check your internet connection and make sure you are signed into the Mac App Store with the Apple Account used for the purchase. Then choose **Plus Pack → Restore Purchases** in the app. If approval is pending, check again after approval. If the problem continues, contact support with the error message.

### Why is the product or price missing from the purchase screen?

Check your internet connection, then select **Reload Store** in the Plus Pack window. If it still does not load after a short wait, reopen the app.

### How do I request a refund?

You can review your purchases and request a refund on [Apple's Report a Problem page](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/). Eligibility is determined through Apple's process.

### How do I change automatic launch at login?

Use **Settings → Launch at Login**. If macOS approval is required, use the menu option to open System Settings and review your login items.

## Contact support

Include your app version, macOS version, Mac model, steps to reproduce the problem, and any error message. Hide personal information in screenshots. Do not send passwords, payment card details, or full purchase receipts.

You can also submit bugs or feature requests that are safe to share publicly through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/hatemogi/DesktopFire/issues). Do not post personal information, such as email addresses or receipts, in public issues.
