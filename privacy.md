# Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-31

**Daehyun Kim** (the “Operator”) handles information in connection with Desktop Fire and support inquiries as described below.

## 1. Information processed by the app

Desktop Fire does not require a separate account and does not collect and transmit your name, email address, contact details, location, screen content, or keyboard input to the Operator's servers. It does not use advertising, user tracking, or behavioral analytics SDKs. The Operator does not run a separate server to collect app usage logs.

The app stores the following settings on your Mac to provide its features:

- Hearth position and size, wood and hearth styles, flame color, and burn duration
- Visibility, window display behavior, and selected display
- Whether the first-run hint has been shown

The app uses the current pointer position and display information on your device to adjust the hearth's transparency and placement. It does not use this information to record screen content or input, or transmit it to the Operator. Launch at login is managed through macOS login items.

## 2. In-app purchases

Plus Pack purchases and restoration are processed through Apple's App Store and StoreKit. The app uses product information and verified transaction information, such as the product identifier, purchase entitlement, and revocation status, to determine whether Plus is available. The app does not transmit this transaction information to the Operator's servers.

The Operator does not receive your payment card number or Apple Account password through the app. Apple's processing of information relating to payments, transaction records, and App Store services is described in [Apple's App Store privacy notice](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/app-store/).

## 3. Support inquiries

When you contact support by email, your sender email address, message, and any files you choose to attach are processed to investigate the issue and respond. Please avoid sending unnecessary personal information.

Support inquiry information is retained for inquiry handling and follow-up for **up to 90 days after the inquiry is resolved**, then deleted. Where retention is required by law, it is kept for the applicable reason and period.

Support inquiries are received and managed through Gmail, provided by Google. Your email address, message, and attachments may be stored and processed on Google’s servers outside your country of residence. For details, see [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Posts on GitHub Issues may be public and displayed with your GitHub account information. GitHub's handling of information is governed by the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## 4. Website visits

The Operator has not added advertising trackers or visitor analytics scripts to these support and marketing pages. When you visit a page hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub logs and stores your IP address for security purposes. See the [GitHub Pages documentation](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages) and [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) for details.

## 5. Sharing and protection

The Operator does not sell your information or use it for personalized advertising. Access to and use of support inquiry information is limited to responding to inquiries and resolving problems. It is not provided to third parties for other purposes, except where required by law.

## 6. Deletion and your rights

You can delete local settings using the app data management methods available on macOS. Settings may remain after uninstalling the app. Contact support if you need help deleting them. Deleting app settings does not delete purchase records managed by Apple.

To request access to, correction or deletion of, or suspension of processing of inquiry information you provided to the Operator, use the contact details below. Requests are handled in accordance with applicable law after identity verification. For public GitHub issues, you can use GitHub's editing, deletion, and support processes.

## 7. Changes and contact

This page and its effective date will be updated if app features or information handling practices change. Where applicable law requires a separate notice, the required procedure will be followed.
