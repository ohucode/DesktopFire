# A little campfire for your Mac

Enjoy a quietly glowing fire in a corner of your workspace while you write, read, or work. **Desktop Fire** is a macOS menu bar app that places firewood and a hearth on your desktop.

## Make room for your own fire

- **Place it where you like** — Drag the hearth into position and choose a size of 50%, 70%, or 100%.
- **Watch the wood change** — Follow the fire from ignition through flames and glowing embers to ash. Choose a burn duration from 30 minutes to 8 hours.
- **Simple controls** — Use the menu bar to show or hide the fire, choose a display, show it on all desktops, and launch at login.
- **Fits into your work** — The fire fades when your pointer lingers nearby and returns when you move away.
- **A silent fireside view** — Enjoy the animation without audio playback.

## Your first fire is free

Oak firewood, the Stone Ring hearth, and the original flame color are free. Relight your fire as often as you like.

For more combinations, try **Desktop Fire Plus**. A one-time purchase unlocks five additional woods, eight additional hearths, and every flame color. There is no subscription. Check the in-app purchase screen for pricing.

## A small scene for your Mac

Requires macOS 14 or later. Available in Korean, English, and Japanese. No separate account is needed, and your position and appearance settings are stored on your Mac.
